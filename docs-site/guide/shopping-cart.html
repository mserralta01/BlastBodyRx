Shop and manage your cart

Build an order with the correct compound strengths and quantities.

1. Browse the catalogue

Select Shop, compare the available compounds, and open the product you want.

2. Add a specific strength

Choose the labeled strength and select Add to cart. The cart opens with the exact item, strength, price, and quantity.

3. Reach the order minimum

Use the quantity controls or continue shopping. The cart shows how much remains before the $100 minimum. Eligible orders receive free U.S. shipping.

4. Continue to checkout

Once the cart reaches $100, select Secure checkout.

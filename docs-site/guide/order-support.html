Orders and support

Find delivery guidance and reach the VirtualFitPath team.

1. Review shipping information

Select Shipping from the footer to review service area, expected handling, and delivery information.

2. Track an order

Select Track order. Use the order information from your confirmation message when tracking becomes available.

3. Contact support

Select Contact or call 800-637-9046. Include the order number and the email used at checkout, but never send payment card details.
